HOW TO PLAY

THE DAY / NIGHT CYCLE

Each day you venture into the Rubble Fields to gather resources, earning Favor. When night falls, waves of predators attack the wall of Rodere. If the wall falls, the city is lost.

Day Phase — Explore, gather resources, carry them to the gate. Spend Favor to recruit troops and build defenses.
Night Phase — Predators swarm toward the wall. Your towers fire automatically. Command your rodents to fight off the attackers. Survive until dawn.

CONTROLS

WASD — Move the Snakeborn
Left Click — Throw a rodent at target (resource, enemy, or location)
Right Click / R — Recall all rodents
C — Charge! All combat rodents swarm nearest enemy
Space — Dodge roll (invincibility frames)
1 2 3 — Select rodent type (Mice / Hares / Shrews)
0 — Select all types
B — Open / close the Build & Recruit shop
F1 — Toggle debug mode
Esc — Pause

YOUR RODENTS

Field Mice — Fast gatherers. Weak fighters. Cheap to recruit (3 Favor).
Hares — Strong soldiers. 5x combat power. Can take hits (10 Favor).
Shrews — Heavy haulers. Carry 3x resources. Slow but essential (6 Favor).

TOWERS & DEFENSES

Press B to open the shop. Build towers near the wall to defend against night raids:

Bolt Tower (20F) — Fast ranged projectiles. Good vs small predators.
Fire Brazier (30F) — Area-of-effect burn damage. Devastating vs groups.
Thorn Wall (15F) — Barrier that slows and damages passing enemies.
War Drum (25F) — Boosts all nearby rodents' attack speed by 50%.

COMBAT

Predators are massive compared to your rodents. Large predators have glowing weak points — aim your throws there for 3x damage. Use the Charge command (C) to swarm enemies with all combat rodents.

When a rodent dies, a soul wisp floats upward. Touch it to "remember" the rodent — free replacement at Rodere. Miss it and replacement costs Favor.

THE WALL

Rodere's wall has hit points. During night attacks, any predator that reaches the wall deals damage to it. If wall HP reaches zero, the city falls and the game is over. Repair the wall (10 Favor for +20 HP) and build towers to keep Rodere standing!
